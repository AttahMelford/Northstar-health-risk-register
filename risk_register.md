**Project type:** Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)  
**Organization:** Fictional healthcare network  
**Scope:** Four hospitals and a regional clinic

## Risk Scoring

- **Inherent Risk Score:** Likelihood × Impact, before considering existing controls.
- **Residual Risk Score:** Risk remaining after considering the effectiveness of existing controls.
- **Risk categories:** Low (1–4), Moderate (5–9), High (10–16), Critical (17–25).

## Important Notes

- Northstar Health Network is a fictional organization created for portfolio demonstration.
- Risk scores and treatment plans are illustrative and should be validated against evidence in a real assessment.
- Existing controls must be verified before concluding that residual risk has been reduced.
- NIST CSF 2.0 references indicate relevant cybersecurity outcomes and should be validated against the framework when used in a real assessment.
